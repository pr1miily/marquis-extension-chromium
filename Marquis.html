Privacy Policy
1. The Marquis's Vow of Discretion

Your data is yours alone. The Marquis works inside your browser and has no server of its own. It does not collect your browsing history or your tool data. A few tools need an outside service to do their job; each is listed below with exactly what it receives.

2. Where Your Data Lives

Workspaces, kanban boards, notes, tasks, favorites, palettes and settings are kept in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local). Screenshots and screen recordings are kept in the extension's own database on this device (IndexedDB). Your theme, weather unit and license key are kept in chrome.storage.sync, so Chrome Sync copies them to your Google account when sync for extensions is on. Uninstalling the extension deletes its local data.

3. Third-Party Couriers

These services are contacted only when you use the tool named, except the network indicator:

  • Weather: open-meteo.com receives the city you search or, if you choose GPS, your coordinates. The radar map loads from windy.com with those coordinates.
  • Text Extractor: ocr.space receives the image or PDF you choose to read.
  • Dictionary: dictionaryapi.dev and datamuse.com receive the word you look up or type.
  • Device Simulator: Google PageSpeed Insights receives the page address you analyze (with your own API key, if you add one).
  • SEO Audit: Google's favicon service receives the domain of the page you audit.
  • License: gumroad.com receives your license key when you activate or re-check it.
  • Feedback: web3forms.com receives your message, your email if you give it, your browser's user agent and the extension version.
  • Support: the ko-fi.com page loads only when you open "A bowl of milk?".
  • Network indicator: while the popup is open it sends a small request to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) every few seconds to measure your connection. No data of yours is included.

Tools that read a web page (SEO, QA, highlighter, Meet attendance) do that work inside your browser. After uninstalling, Chrome opens a short feedback page.

4. Analytics & Tracking

The Marquis employs no hidden trackers, no analytics engines, and no telemetry. Your workflow remains entirely undisturbed and unmonitored.
